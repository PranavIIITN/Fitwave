What is Body Mass Index?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a widely used scale to assess an individual's body weight in relation to their height, serving as an indicator of overall health. It is calculated by dividing a person's weight in kilograms by the square of their height in meters (BMI = weight / (height * height)). The resulting BMI value categorizes individuals into different weight status categories, such as underweight, normal weight, overweight, and obese. BMI provides a quick and simple way to screen for potential health risks associated with weight, although it may not account for factors like muscle mass or body composition in athletes or those with high muscle density.
